# WeBothPlay Hook Library

90 opening hooks for TikTok (and their Shorts/Reels versions), grouped by pattern. Written 2026-10-03 for the 13-week plan (2026-10-05 → 2027-01-03).

**Citations.** "(research §3.3)" = a section of `marketing/TIKTOK_RESEARCH_2026.md`; "(research Q5.2)" = finding 2 under Q5 in its §2.

**Vocabulary used here.** Pillars: P1 Pain point · P2 Instant demo · P3 Squad stats (demo data) · P4 Roulette · P5 Pile of shame (backlog) · P6 Already own it (recommendations) · P7 Group chat energy (memes) · P8 Feature drops · P9 Steam moments (sales/events, dates verified first) · P10 Build in public (rare) · P11 Ask the squad (community prompts). Series: Overlap Reveal, Spin It, Pile of Shame, One Copy Away, Carry Report, Group Chat Energy, Ask the Squad, Tonight's Pick.

## 0. The rules every hook follows

| Rule | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Frame 1 shows the payoff**: the count, the landed pick, the zero hours. No logo intro, no slow pan. | Typical watch time per view is single-digit seconds (entertaining content: about 6 → 9 s) | research §3.3 #1, Q2.2 |
| **Motion within the first second**: counter, reel tick or cursor zoom. | Stops the scroll | research §3.3 #3 |
| **Key message on screen by 3 s.** | Over 63% of TikTok's highest-CTR ads show their key message or product in the first 3 s | research §3.3 #2, Q2.3 |
| **On-screen hook: 3–7 words, at most 2 lines**, in the hook band (y 220→700), ≤26 characters per line | Legible on a phone, clear of TikTok's UI | research §3.3 #5, §3.4 |
| **Spoken line: ≤10 words**, with the video's one searchable phrase said within 5 s | Search starts in-app: 1 in 4 users search within 30 s of opening TikTok | research §3.3 #4, Q4.1 |
| **The same phrase on screen early and in caption line 1.** Put it in the hook when it fits; otherwise in the first caption | Keyword in caption, hashtags and on-screen text; first on-screen text is a keyword spot | research §3.5, Q3.11 |
| **Open loops pay off.** If you tease the result, show it. Never a fake "part 2". | Integrity and trust | research §3.3 #6 |
| **Demo data is labelled on screen** for as long as it's visible: "Demo data · fictional friend group" | Never present synthetic results as real users | research §3.3 #7, §3.11 #3 |
| **Run each pattern at least 3 times before judging it.** | Small samples mislead | research §3.3 #8 |

**Searchable phrases (pick one per video):** "games to play with friends" · "co-op games on steam" · "steam games in common" · "compare steam libraries" · "what game should we play" · "steam backlog" · "multiplayer games steam" · "steam game roulette".

## 2. Demo facts and voice

**Demo group (fictional players, real games).** Use these numbers, or others you can read on the site's demo results yourself: 39 games all four own · 70 between them · 80% overlap · most played together: Counter-Strike 2 · everyone owns Bloons TD 6, nobody has launched it · Nova carries Marvel Rivals (36 h, others 0) · one copy away: Baldur's Gate 3 (Juno is missing it) · 13 games nobody has really played.

**Voice rule for demo data:** third person only ("they", "this group", "Nova"). Never "we", "us" or "my friends" over demo data. Hooks marked **●** below need the demo label. Hooks marked **R** need a real group with written consent from every member, with IDs blurred.

**Title rule:** game titles appear as text or inside real UI recordings. Never use a game's key art or footage as the hero. Never hero the Microsoft-owned titles in the demo group (Sea of Thieves, Forza Horizon 5, Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition, Overwatch 2) (research §3.11 #2, Q6.8).

## 4. Hooks to AVOID

TikTok prohibits fake engagement. Secondary sources report that like-for-like, false incentives, follow-for-follow and vote-baiting ("like if you agree") can make content ineligible for the For You feed (research Q5.1–Q5.2). Comments are falling while shares rise (research Q5.8). Earn shares; don't beg for them.

### 4.1 Engagement bait

| Don't | Why | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| "Like if your group does this" | Vote-baiting | "What does your group always fall back to?" |
| "Comment GAMES and I'll send the link" | Comment-for-reward incentive | Pinned how-to comment (`CTA_LIBRARY.md` §8) |
| "Follow me and I'll follow back" / like-for-like | Prohibited reciprocity | Nothing. Let series earn follows |
| "Tag 3 friends who never pick" | Quota-driven engagement | "Who's the friend who never picks?" |
| "Share this or your squad breaks up" | False stakes | "Send the vote link to your group" (a real action, used sparingly) |
| Giveaways for likes, follows or comments | Prohibited (research §3.11 #5) | None in this plan |

### 4.2 Fake part 2s and withheld payoffs

| Don't | Do instead |
|---|---|
| "Follow for part 2" when part 2 isn't made and scheduled | Only promise a part 2 that is already scheduled, and name the day |
| "Wait for it…" and the spin is never shown, or cut before it lands | Show the landing in frame 1 (flash-forward) and again at the end |
| "Guess the overlap" with the answer hidden | "Here's their overlap: 39. What's yours?" (the payoff stays in frame 1) |
| "You won't believe what it landed on" | Show what it landed on |

### 4.3 Misleading claims

| Don't | Why | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| "The official way to compare Steam libraries" / "Steam's secret feature" | Implies Valve endorsement (research Q6.5–Q6.6) | "Compare steam libraries in one link" |
| "Steam can't do this" | Unverified, and attacks a platform (matrix §6.1) | Describe what WeBothPlay does |
| "Works with private profiles" | It can't read private libraries | "See who's private and send the fix" |
| "Free forever", "no catch" | Premium exists | "Free to compare" |
| "Co-op games for 6 players" | Steam has no max-player data (JTBD F4) | "Games your whole group owns" |
| "In 5 seconds" / "in 30 seconds" | Only true if the video's own clock proves it | Show it happening, uncut |
| "Saves your group hours" | No data | Show the pick happening |
| "My friends and I share 39 games" | Demo data presented as real | "This group shares 39" with the label |
| "You own these games" | You don't know that | "You might already own" |
| Prices, sale dates, release dates, event dates you haven't verified | Unverified (research §4, §5) | Verify that day, or leave them out |
| "AI picks your game" | Not how the product works | "Roulette", "Best for tonight", "Group vote" |

### 4.4 Brand and IP risk

- Any hook built on a game's trailer, key art or gameplay clip. That includes Microsoft-owned titles as hero content (research Q6.8, §3.11 #2).
- Steam's logo, Steam's UI sounds or a Steam-look layout (research §3.11 #1).
- Fear or rage bait ("Steam is hiding this from you"), console-war bait, or jokes at a real person's or streamer's expense.
- Real Steam names, avatars or IDs without written consent (research §3.11 #3).

## 5. Write your own

**Formula:** frame 1 = the payoff visual · on-screen = 3–7 words with a number, a name or a conflict · spoken = ≤10 words carrying one searchable phrase · caption line 1 = the same phrase plus the value.

**Checklist before you use a new hook**
- [ ] Does frame 1 already show the result?
- [ ] Is there motion before 1 s, and is the message readable by 3 s?
- [ ] 3–7 words on screen, ≤26 characters per line, ≤2 lines?
- [ ] ≤10 spoken words, with the phrase said within 5 s?
- [ ] Is every number from the demo facts (§2) or from your own consenting group?
- [ ] Third person for demo data? Label planned?
- [ ] No bait, no fake part 2, no Valve implication, no unverified claim?
- [ ] Logged with a pattern code (§1) so it counts toward the 3-post minimum?

**Turning a winner into variations** (`WEEKLY_OPTIMIZATION_PLAYBOOK.md` §4): keep the pattern and the frame-1 visual type, and change one thing at a time: the fact (39 → 80% → 13), the friend (Nova → Juno), or the searchable phrase.
