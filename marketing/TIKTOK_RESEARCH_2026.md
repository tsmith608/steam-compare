# TikTok Research 2026: WeBothPlay

Prepared: 2026-10-03 · Planning window: 2026-10-05 to 2027-01-04 (13 weeks)
Product: WeBothPlay (webothplay.com): compare friends' Steam libraries, see shared games, Game Roulette, Backlog Slayer, co-op/multiplayer filters, Discord bot.
Constraint: a solo owner, no daily talking-head filming. Content comes from screen recordings, motion graphics, typography, memes, caption-led edits, off-camera voiceover, and clearly labelled demo data.

## 1. Method & source-quality notes

**How the research was done.** Web searches ran on 2026-10-03, in "standard" mode by default and "extended" mode for recent or niche facts. Primary sources were preferred (TikTok Creator Academy, TikTok Ads Help Center, TikTok Next 2026 report, TikTok Newsroom, Valve/Steamworks, Microsoft/Xbox). Behind those came studies with stated methods (Buffer, Metricool, Socialinsider, Dash Social, Rival IQ/Quid, Fanpage Karma), then trade press (Search Engine Land, Social Media Today, TechCrunch, MediaPost).

**Important limitations. Read before relying on this document:**
1. **No pages could be opened directly.** The session's network egress proxy blocked WebFetch and curl for every domain tried: tiktok.com, ads.tiktok.com, support.tiktok.com, developers.tiktok.com, newsroom.tiktok.com, buffer.com, metricool.com, socialmediatoday.com, searchenginejournal.com, partner.steamgames.com, store.steampowered.com, steamcommunity.com and en.wikipedia.org. Every finding therefore comes from **search-engine extracts** of the cited pages, not from reading them in full. Numbers are given as the extracts reported them.
2. **The session-wide web-search quota (200 calls) ran out mid-research.** This left Q8–Q12 partly unresearched and the **event calendar almost entirely unverified**. Anything taken from background knowledge rather than a source retrieved this session is tagged **[Unverified]** and listed in §5 (Verification backlog).
3. Dates: 2026 sources come first, then sources from the last 12–18 months. Anything older is tagged **[Older]**.

**Labels used**
- **[Fact]**: stated by the cited source, as surfaced in its extract.
- **[Observation]**: a pattern or conflict seen across sources.
- **[Interpretation]**: my inference or recommendation.
- **[Unverified]**: background knowledge that could not be checked this session. Verify before acting on it.
- **[Older]**: source more than 18 months old (before about April 2025).
- **Tier**: 1 = TikTok/Valve/Microsoft primary · 2 = study with a stated method · 3 = trade press · 4 = vendor or SEO blog (used only to triangulate).

## 2. Findings by research question

### Q1. Posting cadence (2025–2026): does daily beat 3–5×/week?

1. **[Fact]** TikTok's Creator Academy says publishers who post several times a day get more video views and more For You feed exposure. It also advises setting "a daily post target you can consistently hit". Source: TikTok Creator Academy, "Posting cadence best practices (publishers)", https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/posting-cadence-best-practices-publishers (no date in the extract; Tier 1). *Caveat: the article is written for media publishers with newsroom-scale output.*
2. **[Observation]** Many 2026 vendor blogs repeat that "TikTok recommends 1–4 posts per day", e.g. https://joinbrands.com/blog/how-often-to-post-on-tiktok/ and https://stackinfluence.com/blog/how-often-you-post-on-tiktok-for-a-business. I could not trace this to a current TikTok page this session. Treat it as legacy guidance and unverified.
3. **[Fact]** Buffer studied 11.4M TikTok posts from 150k+ accounts. Its fixed-effects regression compares each account with itself over time. Against 1 post/week:
   - 2–5 posts/week gave up to +17% views per post.
   - 6–10 posts/week gave up to +29%.
   - 11+ posts/week gave up to +34%.

   The steepest efficiency gain is the step from 1 to 2–5 posts/week. Going from 5 to 10 adds only about 12 more points. **Median** views per post barely moved (about 489 at 1/week vs about 459 at 11+/week). The gain therefore comes from more chances at outlier hits, not from a better typical post. Source: Buffer, https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/ (published 2025-10-08 per coverage). Coverage: https://www.searchenginejournal.com/study-shows-2-5-weekly-tiktoks-deliver-biggest-view-increase/558641/ and https://www.digitalinformationworld.com/2025/10/the-real-posting-sweet-spot-on-tiktok.html. Tier 2.
4. **[Fact]** Dash Social studied 1,360 global brands from 2025-07-01 to 2025-12-31. Brands posting **fewer than 6 times/week** had higher engagement than higher-volume posters: **+63% on TikTok** and +13% on Instagram. The For You Page's share of TikTok views rose from **31% (2023) to 58% (2025)**. Sources: Dash Social 2026 press releases via GlobeNewswire/AAP, https://www.aap.com.au/aapreleases/globenewswire9708696/ and https://www.aap.com.au/aapreleases/globenewswire9647819/ (2026; the extract did not show which release contains which figure); report PDF https://pages.dashsocial.com/hubfs/social-media-benchmark/2026/H1/tiktok.pdf. Tier 2. *Engagement rate and views per post are different metrics. Findings 3 and 4 can both be true.*
5. **[Fact]** Socialinsider's 2026 benchmarks cover 70M brand posts from Jan 2024 to Dec 2025:
   - Brands average about **5 posts/week** on TikTok.
   - TikTok engagement rate is 2.60% by followers (−10% YoY) and 4.20% by views (+9% YoY).
   - Comments per post fell 24% YoY; **shares per post rose 45% YoY**.

   Sources: https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks and https://cdn.socialinsider.io/documents/2026_social_media_benchmarks.pdf (2026). Tier 2.
6. **[Fact]** Rival IQ/Quid's 2026 Social Media Industry Benchmark Report found brands publish about **2.0 TikTok videos/week**, against 3.7 posts/week on Instagram. TikTok had the highest engagement rate of any platform at 2.01%. Source: https://www.quid.com/knowledge-hub/resource-library/blog/2026-social-media-industry-benchmark-report (2026). Tier 2.
7. **[Fact]** Metricool's TikTok Study 2026 covered 2,314,756 posts from 92k+ accounts. The average video got **32,895 views, down 31% YoY**. Accounts published about 80% more content (videos +72%, image/carousel posts +140%). Metricool attributes the view decline mainly to saturation. Sources: https://metricool.com/tiktok-study/ and https://metricool.com/press-release-tiktok-study-2026/ (2026 edition; exact release date not captured). Tier 2.
8. **[Fact, secondary]** Hootsuite's 2025 data reportedly recommends 3–5 TikTok posts/week for businesses, which averaged about 3.7/week. This is cited via https://www.socialcrawl.dev/blog/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok; Hootsuite's original was not accessed. Tier 4.
9. **[Interpretation]** The evidence points the same way. **3–5 posts/week captures most of the per-post gain.** Posting daily or more adds total views, mostly through more chances at a hit. It comes with a lower engagement rate per post and rising saturation. No small- or new-account-specific causal study was found. Buffer's within-account model is the best available evidence and applies to all account sizes. For a solo owner, the binding constraint is **sustainable quality**, not raw frequency.

### Q2. Video length, retention and TikTok's own creative guidance

1. **[Fact] [Older, Mar 2025]** Buffer analysed 1.1M TikToks:
   - 86% of posted videos run under 60 s.
   - Videos over 60 s got ≥43.2% more reach and 63.8% more watch time than shorter ones.
   - Videos over 60 s got 70.3% more reach than 10–30 s videos and 95.7% more than 5–10 s clips.
   - 3–10-minute videos had the highest average views.
   - Distribution: 33.7% are 10–30 s, 27.3% are 30–60 s, 22.2% are 5–10 s, 12.3% are over 1 min.

   Buffer itself says length is unlikely to be make-or-break, and that hook and quality matter more. Sources: https://buffer.com/resources/longer-tiktoks-get-more-views-data/ and https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/research-tiktok-longer-videos-get-more-reach/742754/ (March 2025). Tier 2. *The data is correlational: longer videos may come from more established creators.*
2. **[Fact]** Dash Social (2026 press materials) says entertaining content extends watch time **from about 6 to about 9 seconds** on TikTok and Instagram. Sources: https://www.aap.com.au/aapreleases/globenewswire9647819/ and https://www.aap.com.au/aapreleases/globenewswire9708696/ (2026). Tier 2. **[Interpretation]** Typical watch time per view is single-digit seconds, so the first 3 seconds decide most outcomes.
3. **[Fact] [date unknown]** TikTok's "Auction Ads Creative Tips" says over **63% of videos with the highest CTR show their key message or product within the first 3 seconds**. Source: https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/Auction_Ads_Creative_Tips.pdf (undated ad-performance data; treat as possibly older than 18 months). Tier 1.
4. **[Fact]** TikTok's ad specs: TopView runs 5–60 s with **9–15 s recommended**. Auction in-feed ads run up to 10 min. Sources: https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-reservation-topview?lang=en and https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-auction-in-feed-ads?lang=en (live help pages). Tier 1. *These are ad formats, not organic guidance.*
5. **[Fact, secondary] [Older]** "88% of TikTok users say sound is vital to the TikTok experience." The figure is widely attributed to TikTok Marketing Science and commonly dated 2021. It came through 2026 vendor creative-best-practice guides (search extract; specific page not identified) and was not re-verified.
6. **[Observation]** No credible public **completion-rate** benchmark turned up. Dash Social publishes retention benchmarks by industry, but only behind an eMarketer login: https://www.emarketer.com/chart/c/367437/worldwide-tiktok-average-retention-rate-by-industry-367437. Vendor-blog figures such as "89% average completion" have no source and were excluded.
7. **[Interpretation]** Length by goal:
   - Hook tests and reach: 9–20 s.
   - Product demo: 20–45 s.
   - Search-led lists and explainers: 45–90 s.
   - 90 s+ only for dense event roundups.

### Q3. Hooks for gaming/PC/Steam content, text safe zones, captions

**Safe zones**
1. **[Fact]** TikTok's Ads Help (In-Feed) says safe-zone size depends on the dimension, the ad caption length and any add-on formats. Key elements such as text and logos must stay inside the safe zone. TikTok offers downloadable safe-zone files: Standard (LTR), Arabic (RTL), and versions with or without anchors. Source: https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-auction-in-feed-ads?lang=en (live). Tier 1.
2. **[Fact]** TopView has two-stage safe zones (open screen, then in-feed). Creative must satisfy both, and the in-feed zone is the stricter one. Source: https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-reservation-topview?lang=en. Tier 1.
3. **[Observation]** TikTok does not appear to publish a single pixel spec in text; it publishes template files. Third-party readings for 1080×1920 vary:

**Hooks and gaming context**

5. **[Fact] [Older, 2021-era Material studies]** TikTok Marketing Science and Material found:
   - 75% of TikTok gamers discovered new gaming content on TikTok.
   - 41% downloaded a featured game after seeing gaming content.
   - 36% made purchases to play.
   - **90% encouraged friends to play** after discovering a game on TikTok.
   - 82% of TikTok users game at least weekly.

   Sources: TikTok for Business, "TikTok made me play it: supercharging game discovery", https://ads.tiktok.com/business/id/blog/tiktok-made-me-play-it-supercharging-game-discovery, and Campaign ME, https://campaignme.com/the-world-of-gaming-on-tiktok/. Tier 1, older.
6. **[Fact] [Older, 2024-02-16]** TikTok says it is the No. 1 platform for discovering new mobile games. Source: Tubefilter, https://tubefilter.com/2024/02/16/tiktok-mobile-game-report/. *Mobile-focused.*
7. **[Fact]** TikTok's Next 2026 trend report sets out three trends:
   - **"Reali-Tea":** audiences want unfiltered, behind-the-scenes and community content. "Comment-driven content continues to outperform polished, one-way messaging." Millennial and Gen Z users are 1.5× more likely to engage with brands that build genuine community.
   - **"Curiosity Detours":** discovery that starts from search.
   - **"Emotional ROI":** lead with *why* to choose something.

   Sources: https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/TikTok_Next_2026_Trend_Report.pdf and https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-tiktok-next-2026-our-trend-forecast-for-marketers-for-the-year-ahead?lang=en (released by Jan 2026). Tier 1.
8. **[Observation]** TikTok has topic pages for discovery-style queries. Examples: https://www.tiktok.com/discover/games-to-play-2026, https://www.tiktok.com/discover/new-games-coming-out-in-2026, https://www.tiktok.com/discover/gaming-news and https://www.tiktok.com/tag/gamediscover. This is a weak signal of search demand for "what to play" phrasing.
9. **[Interpretation]** Hook patterns that fit WeBothPlay. These are untested hypotheses for the first 4 weeks of A/B testing:
   - **Number reveal:** "4 friends. 1,214 games. Only 23 in common." (demo data, labelled)
   - **Backlog shame:** "Games ALL of you own that NOBODY has played."
   - **Roulette commitment:** "Whatever the wheel lands on, we play tonight." Deliver the payoff at the end.
   - **Group-chat meme:** "The group chat at 9pm: 'so what are we playing?'"
   - **Search-led list:** "Co-op games for 4 players you probably already own."
   - **Problem/solution:** "Stop buying a game only 2 of your 5 friends have."
   - **Event-led:** "Next Fest co-op demos worth trying with your group" or "Winter Sale: one game the whole group can play".

**Captions and on-screen text**

10. **[Fact, secondary]** Vendor guides say to keep captions within about 100 characters before the "See more" cut-off in non-CJK languages. This came from a search extract of 2026 "TikTok creative best practices" guides; the specific page was not identified. Put the keyword and value in the first ~100 characters.
11. **[Fact]** Sprout Social's 2026 TikTok SEO guide recommends putting the keyword in the caption, the hashtags and the on-screen text. Source: https://sproutsocial.com/insights/tiktok-seo/ (2026). Tier 2/4.
12. **[Unverified mechanism]** Vendor guides claim TikTok indexes spoken words (speech recognition) and on-screen text (OCR), e.g. https://almcorp.com/blog/tiktok-seo/. No TikTok primary page confirming this was retrieved. **[Interpretation]** Saying, showing and writing the keyword costs nothing, so do all three.

### Q4. TikTok search/SEO and hashtag strategy

1. **[Fact]** From TikTok Next 2026:
   - **1 in 4 TikTok users start searching within 30 seconds of opening the app.**
   - **2 in 3 TikTok searchers** say a central reason they search on TikTok is discovering useful things beyond what they were looking for. This comes from TikTok Marketing Science's 2025 Future of Search study with WARC.

   Source: https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/TikTok_Next_2026_Trend_Report.pdf. Tier 1.
2. **[Fact]** **Keyword management for video metadata** (reported April 2026). Creators can review the search keywords TikTok assigns automatically, block irrelevant ones and suggest more relevant terms. TikTok reviews suggestions before applying them. Coverage frames the rollout as US-first. Sources: Social Media Today, https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-lets-users-add-relevant-keywords-to-metadata/818507/; Uppbeat, https://uppbeat.io/blog/creator-news/tiktok-now-lets-you-control-the-search-keywords-on-your-videos; Neil Patel, https://neilpatel.com/blog/tiktok-keyword-metadata/. All April 2026; Tier 3. *Availability may vary by account and region.*
3. **[Fact]** **TikTok caps posts at 5 hashtags.** Users saw it through an in-app notice; TikTok did not formally announce it. The notice cited less clutter, less spam and better relevance. Users noticed the cap in July–August 2025. Sources: Search Engine Land, https://searchengineland.com/tiktok-limits-hashtags-460894 (2025-08-19); MediaPost, https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/408371/tiktok-limits-hashtags-as-algorithms-become-more-s.html (2025-08-22); Social Media Today, https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-implements-five-hashtag-limit-per-post/757857/. Tier 3.
4. **[Fact]** **Instagram also caps posts and Reels at 5 hashtags**, announced Dec 2025 with a gradual rollout. Mosseri's position is that a few specific tags beat long generic lists. Sources: https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-hashtags-five-per-post-10923075 and https://www.blogdumoderateur.com/instagram-limite-hashtags-5-par-publication/ (Dec 2025). Tier 3.
5. **[Fact]** TikTok's Community Guidelines update (announced 2025-08-15, effective 2025-09-13) says search results and recommendations "may look different for everyone". They are personalised using past searches and watch history. Sources: TechCrunch, https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/15/tiktoks-new-guidelines-add-subtle-changes-for-live-creators-ai-content-and-more/; TikTok Newsroom, https://newsroom.tiktok.com/community-guidelines-update-newsroom. Tier 1/3.
6. **[Observation]** No 2025–2026 study with a stated method on hashtag *breadth* (broad vs niche) was retrieved. The 5-hashtag cap makes "how many" largely moot.
7. **[Interpretation]** Search now drives discovery more than hashtags do. Write every video around one **searchable phrase**, e.g. "co-op games to play with friends", "games we all own on Steam", "what to play with friends tonight" or "Steam Winter Sale co-op".

### Q5. Comments, reply videos, Stitch/Duet, and engagement bait

1. **[Fact]** TikTok's Community Guidelines (Integrity & Authenticity) prohibit fake engagement: buying followers, likes, views or comments, and using bots, scripts or automation to generate activity. TikTok strips inauthentic metrics. Penalties escalate through restrictions, then suspensions, then bans. Sources: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity (live; read via extracts); summary at https://www.auditsocials.com/blog/tiktok-fake-artificial-engagement-policy-2026-bots-bought-followers-account-risk. Tier 1/4.
2. **[Observation, exact wording unverified]** Secondary sources consistently report that the following can make content **ineligible for the For You feed** under Integrity & Authenticity:
   - "like-for-like" promises;
   - false incentives to engage;
   - "follow me and I'll follow back";
   - vote-baiting ("like if you agree").

   Sources: https://www.auditsocials.com/platforms/tiktok-community-guidelines and https://www.dashsocial.com/blog/tiktok-community-guidelines. *Verify the current wording on TikTok's page (§5).*
3. **[Fact]** The Aug 2025 update (effective 2025-09-13) made three relevant changes:
   - Creators must **disclose commercial content**.
   - **In markets where TikTok Shop operates, content that directs users to buy off-platform gets reduced visibility.**
   - Comment sections are ranked per user.

   Sources: TechCrunch (above); Shopifreaks, https://www.shopifreaks.com/tiktok-to-reduce-visibility-for-off-platform-commercial-content/ (Aug 2025). Tier 1/3.
4. **[Unverified, vendor-reported]** A newer Guidelines version was reportedly released 2026-08-25 and took effect 2026-09-24. Reported changes:
   - Undisclosed commercial content now gets reduced visibility, or the disclosure setting switched on automatically, rather than automatic FYF ineligibility.
   - An explicit warning → restriction → ban ladder.
   - Bans can extend to associated accounts.
   - AI-labelling rules are unchanged.

   Sources: https://www.usefastlane.ai/blog/tiktok-community-guidelines, https://www.storika.ai/guides/tiktok-community-guidelines-update-2026-brand-risk and https://socialday.live/features/tiktoks-september-community-guidelines-formalise-off-platform-account-bans. **Not confirmed against https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines this session.**
5. **[Fact]** Buffer's State of Social Media Engagement 2026 covers 52M+ posts on 10 platforms (published about March 2026). Accounts that **reply to comments** consistently outperform those that don't on all six platforms with reply data: Threads, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, X and Bluesky. **TikTok was not among them.** TikTok's median engagement was about 4.6% in 2025, roughly +3% YoY. Sources: https://buffer.com/resources/state-of-social-media-engagement-2026/ and https://www.netinfluencer.com/replying-to-comments-boosts-social-media-engagement-across-all-major-platforms-data-shows/. Tier 2.
6. **[Fact]** TikTok Next 2026 says comment-driven content outperforms polished one-way messaging (Q3 #7). Socialinsider found comments per post −24% and shares per post +45% YoY (Q1 #5).
7. **[Unverified]** Reply-to-comment-with-video, Stitch (clips of up to about 5 s from another video) and Duet are standard TikTok features. Their current limits were not re-checked.
8. **[Interpretation]** Because shares are rising and comments falling, design for **"send this to the group chat" moments**: relatable group-gaming pain and a useful list. Don't beg. Ask real questions tied to the content ("Which of these would your group actually finish?"). Avoid "like/follow if…", "comment X to get Y", and "follow for part 2" unless part 2 is already made and scheduled.

### Q6. Audio policy, music licensing, game footage and Steam brand rules

**Music**
1. **[Fact]** TikTok's Commercial Music Library (CML) is a pre-cleared library of about 1M tracks that businesses can use free. It covers:
   - **Who can use it:** businesses "cannot use the general music library for commercial usage". **Business accounts only see "Commercial Sounds"** when adding a sound.
   - **Personal accounts:** using a personal account to promote a business risks copyright infringement, because general-library songs are not cleared for commercial content, defined as "any content posted by a brand".

   Sources: https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/commercial-music-library, https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-use-the-commercial-music-library and https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/audio-library-royalty-free-music (live). Tier 1. **This confirms that business accounts cannot use most trending (general-library) music.**
2. **[Fact, secondary]** The CML licence covers TikTok only. Re-using those tracks on YouTube, Instagram or broadcast is not covered. Sources: https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/tiktok-music-library-explained and https://www.foximusic.com/blog/commercial-music-licensing-tiktok-guide/ (2026). Tier 4; consistent with Tier 1 framing.
3. **[Fact, secondary]** TikTok's disclosure toggle offers two options:
   - **"Your brand"** for promoting your own business, labelled "Promotional content".
   - **"Branded content"** for third-party paid promotion, labelled "Paid partnership".

   Undisclosed branded content may lose FYF eligibility. The 2026 version reportedly reduces reach instead (Q5 #4). Source: https://emplicit.co/tiktok-paid-partnership-settings-guide/. Tier 4; disclosure duty confirmed in Tier 1/3 coverage of the 2025 update.
4. **[Observation]** No 2025–2026 study with a stated method comparing trending vs original vs voiceover audio was retrieved (search quota ran out). For a business account the choice is CML vs original audio in any case.

   - Valve may require changes or approve communications that use the brand.

   Sources: https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/branding (live) and the PDF revision https://cdn.cloudflare.steamstatic.com/steamcommunity/public/images/steamworks_docs/english/Steam_Guidelines_060222.pdf (2017 text). Tier 1. **[Interpretation]** WeBothPlay is not a Steamworks partner, so it has *no* licence to use the logo. Use the word "Steam" only descriptively ("for your Steam library").

## 3. Decisions for WeBothPlay

### 3.1 Cadence
- **Baseline: 4 videos/week**, spread evenly (e.g. Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun), giving about 52 videos over 13 weeks. This sits in the 2–5/week band where Buffer found the steepest per-post gain and below the 6/week line where Dash Social saw higher engagement.
- **Stretch: 7/week (daily)** only in event weeks once dates are confirmed (§4): Next Fest, Halloween/Scream Fest, Thanksgiving–Cyber Monday week (+ Autumn Sale if confirmed), Game Awards week, Winter Sale opening week. About 5 stretch weeks add roughly 15 videos.
- **Floor: 3/week.** Never go 7+ days without a post.
- **Optional:** +1 Photo Mode carousel/week (save-worthy list). It counts as a bonus, not toward the baseline.
- **Decision gates.**
  - **Week 4:** if production feels sustainable and views per post are stable or rising, try 5–6/week through week 8.
  - **Week 8:** keep the higher rate only if total views and follows per 1k views improved without hurting average watch time.

### 3.2 Length mix
| Bucket | Share | Length | Typical content |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quick hits | 50% | 9–20 s | Meme/caption-led, number reveal, roulette spin, one idea, loopable |
| Demos & lists | 35% | 25–45 s | Screen-recorded feature demo; "5 co-op games your group already owns" |
| Deep dives | 15% | 60–120 s | Event roundups (Next Fest co-op demos, Winter Sale group picks); tests Buffer's >60 s reach correlation |

### 3.3 Hook rules
1. **Frame 1 shows the payoff**: overlap number, roulette landing or a Backlog Slayer reveal. No logo intro, no slow pan.
2. **Key message on screen by 3 s** (TikTok ads guidance: 63% of top-CTR videos do this).
3. **Motion within the first second**: number counter, wheel spin or cursor zoom.
4. **One searchable phrase per video.** Use it in the hook text, say it within 5 s, and put it in the first line of the caption.
5. **3–7 word hook text**, at most 2 lines, inside the hook band (§3.4).
6. **Open loops must pay off.** If you tease the wheel result, show it. Never use a fake "part 2".
7. **Label demo data in-frame** for the whole time it is visible: "Demo data · fictional friend group".
8. **Rotate the hook patterns from Q3 #9.** Run each pattern at least 3 times before judging it.

### 3.4 Text safe zones (1080×1920)
- **Critical-content box: x 64→940 px, y 150→1436 px** (876×1286). This is the conservative union of third-party measurements of TikTok's templates. It keeps clear of the right action rail (140 px) and the bottom caption/CTA area (484 px).
- **Hook band:** y 220→700. **Subtitle/body band:** y 900→1400. Leave room for TikTok auto-captions if you enable them.
- **Never essential:** the top 150 px, the bottom 484 px and the right 140 px. The CTA end card stays inside the box.
- **Type (interpretation):** hook 60–80 px font size, body 40–48 px, ≤26 characters per line, heavy weight with a stroke or shadow for contrast.
- **Process:** download TikTok's official safe-zone template from the Ads Help in-feed spec page and keep it as a guide layer. Preview in TikTok Studio before scheduling. Long captions push the overlay higher, so keep captions short.
- **Screen-capture tip:** record the site in a 1080×1920 browser viewport (e.g. a 360×640 CSS-pixel device at 3× DPR) so UI text stays readable without heavy cropping.

### 3.5 Hashtags and keywords
- **3–5 hashtags; hard cap 5.**
  - 1 broad (#steam or #pcgaming).
  - 2 intent tags (#coopgames, #gamestoplaywithfriends, #multiplayergames, #steamgames).
  - 0–1 event tag (#steamnextfest, #steamsale, #steamwintersale, used only when relevant and once dates are confirmed).
  - 0–1 brand tag (#webothplay).
  - *Tag volumes were not checked in Creative Center this session.*
- **Keyword placement:** first words of the caption + first on-screen text + spoken within 5 s.
- **Use TikTok's keyword management** (if your account has it) to remove irrelevant auto-assigned keywords and suggest "co-op games", "games to play with friends" and similar.
- **Track** each video's share of Search traffic in TikTok Studio, and promote the phrases that win into recurring series titles.

### 3.6 Audio policy (business account)
- Use a **Business Account** (CML access, analytics, web scheduler). Use **CML sounds or original audio only**.
- **Default stack:** off-camera voiceover (your voice or TikTok's built-in text-to-speech) + UI SFX + a CML music bed at low volume.
- **Never** rip trending songs into an edit or use unlicensed music. A business account can't access the general library, and using it for promotion risks infringement.
- **Cross-posting:** export a version with no CML track and replace it with music licensed for all platforms, or SFX/voiceover only.
- **Disclosure:** turn on **"Your brand" (Promotional content)** for any post that demos or promotes WeBothPlay.
- **AI:** if you use realistic AI voice clones or AI visuals of people or scenes, apply TikTok's AI-generated content label.

### 3.7 CTA frequency
- **Every video:** the product is visible in the demo, and a small "webothplay.com" end card (≤2 s, inside the safe box) closes it.
- **Explicit CTA on about 1 in 3 videos** (1–2/week): "Compare your group's Steam libraries free — link in bio."
- **The other videos** end with a genuine content question instead of a CTA.
- **Pinned comment** on demo videos with a one-line how-to.
- **Discord bot CTA** about twice a month: "Add the bot, type /roulette".
- **No premium or purchase CTAs in organic posts** (TikTok Shop markets reduce visibility of off-platform purchase content). Say "free to compare", because premium tiers exist.
- **Bio link** with UTM parameters, e.g. `webothplay.com/?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=bio`, to measure site visits and Discord installs. First confirm the account can show a bio link (Q9).

### 3.8 Posting-time method
1. **Weeks 1–4:** rotate 3 slots in the main audience's time zone: 12:00, 17:00 and 20:00. 20:00 sits in Metricool's 6–9 pm window. Give each slot equal numbers of each video bucket.
2. **Compare per slot:** views at 24 h and 72 h, average watch time, % watched in full, follows per 1k views, and traffic-source mix.
3. **Week 5 onward:** lock in the best 2 slots (±1 h). Re-test one alternative slot every 4 weeks.
4. Check TikTok Studio's audience-activity view (if shown) for the main region and time zone.

### 3.9 Scheduling workflow (safest for a solo owner)
1. **Weekly batch (one session):** script → produce 4–7 videos from reusable templates (Figma/After Effects/CapCut). Sources: screen recordings and demo-data generator output.
2. **QA checklist per video:**
   - safe-zone overlay passed;
   - demo-data label present;
   - CML or original audio only;
   - "Your brand" disclosure on;
   - ≤5 hashtags;
   - keyword in caption line 1;
   - no Steam logo;
   - capsule art incidental only.
3. **Publish through first-party or audited channels only:**
   - **TikTok Studio (web) scheduler**, 7–10 days ahead (verify the window), or
   - an official TikTok-partner scheduler (Buffer, Later, Hootsuite, Metricool or Sprout) using TikTok's Content Posting API. Use "notify, finish in app" for anything needing in-app features.
4. **Building your own uploader:** expect TikTok app review plus a Content Posting API audit. Unaudited clients post private-only (verify in the developer docs).
5. **Officially automatable:** scheduled publishing. **Not automatable:** likes, follows, comments, DMs, view or follower purchases, engagement pods, multi-account reposting, and browser bots on tiktok.com. All of these breach Integrity & Authenticity.
6. **Daily 10–15 min, by hand:** reply to comments. Make 1–2 reply-with-video posts per week from the best comments; these can count toward the baseline.

### 3.10 Repurposing rules
- Master in your editor at 1080×1920 with no TikTok watermark, and keep core cuts ≤60 s.
- For Shorts and Reels: swap the audio (CML is TikTok-only), re-check each platform's UI safe zones, rewrite captions, and use ≤5 hashtags on Instagram.
- Post natively on each platform under one official account per platform. Don't mass-repost identical files across multiple accounts.
- Verify current Shorts and Reels length limits and watermark guidance before relying on 3-minute cuts (Q12 is unverified).

### 3.11 Compliance and IP guardrails
1. **Steam/Valve**
   - No Steam logo anywhere: avatar, cover, end card.
   - Use "Steam" only descriptively.
   - Never imply partnership ("official", "Steam's tool").
   - Put "Not affiliated with Valve. Steam is a trademark of Valve Corporation." in the bio and site footer, and in captions when Steam is prominent.
2. **Game art and footage**
   - Capsule art only as it naturally appears in real UI recordings: small, many titles, never one game's key art as the cover.
   - No publisher logos as hero elements.
   - **No Microsoft-owned game footage or art as hero content** (Xbox/Bethesda/Activision Blizzard).
   - Avoid trailers and gameplay clips in promos. Prefer motion graphics, typography and your own UI.
   - Remove content promptly if a rights-holder objects.
3. **Demo data and privacy**
   - Label synthetic friend groups on screen whenever they appear.
   - Never present synthetic results as real users.
   - Never show real Steam names, avatars or profile URLs without written consent.
   - Blur IDs in any real recording.
4. **Claim accuracy (action item)**
   - The repo states different group sizes. `src/app/components/CleanDataEntryForm.jsx` says "Up to 4 Players". `FeatureDemoSection.jsx` says "up to 10 friends". `HighlightsSection.jsx` says "up to 10 players". The brief says 2–12.
   - **Align product copy and the real limit before any video states a number.**
   - Say "free to compare" (core is free; premium is $3.99/$9.99 per month).
5. **Engagement integrity**
   - No like/follow bait, no false incentives, no giveaways tied to engagement.
   - Deliver every promised payoff or part 2.
6. **Disclosure and AI**
   - "Your brand" toggle on for promotional posts.
   - AI label on realistic synthetic media.
7. **Music**: CML or original audio only on TikTok, and re-licensed audio elsewhere.

### 3.12 Measurement (lightweight)
- **Per video:** average watch time, % full watches, 24/72 h views, shares, follows per 1k views, and traffic-source split (For You / Search / Profile / Following).
- **Weekly:** bio-link sessions (UTM), new comparisons started, Discord installs.
- **Kill or keep:** drop a hook pattern after 3 posts below the account median. Double down on any format that beats the median by 2× twice.
